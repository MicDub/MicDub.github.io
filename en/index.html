Overtake it!

Racing game · Android and browser

A racing game about overtaking: long two-lane country roads, a truck ahead, oncoming traffic, and one second where the other lane is clear. 30 tracks, rain, fog and snow, a track designer, and replays with TV cameras.

Spitskaart

Website · live traffic map (Dutch)

A live map of traffic on Dutch roads, updated every minute with open data from the NDW. See how much congestion there is, how that compares to a normal weekday, and how long your daily commute takes right now.

spitskaart.nl

Max Speed

App · Android

Shows the speed limit of the road you are driving on next to your own speed, and warns you with sound and visuals when you go too fast. Based on your GPS position and OpenStreetMap.

History

Dubbeldam Software has been here before: from 1994 to 2013 the company made software for guitar players and web developers. DsTune and DsChordFinder were well reviewed in computer and guitar magazines and sold all over the world, exclusively over the Internet.

In 2026 Dubbeldam Software was founded again under the same name. The old programs can still be downloaded for free.

  • “DsChordFinder: music to your ears”, Computer Shopper, November 1996
  • Computer!Totaal, December 1994 (ChordFinder 1.0)
  • PC Format, April 1996 (DsTune and DsChordFinder)
  • ‘Shop of the Month’, Guitar The Magazine, March 1997
  1. 1994 Dubbeldam Software founded in Delft, ChordFinder 1.0
  2. 1995 Smooth Editor, a smooth-scrolling DOS text editor
  3. 1996 DsTune and DsChordFinder in PC Format and Computer Shopper
  4. 2003 HtmlTweak and DOMSpy for web developers
  5. 2011 Guitar Music Analyzer for Android
  6. 2013 Last update of the old website
  7. 2016 Max Speed for Android
  8. 2026 Founded again: Overtake it! and Spitskaart
